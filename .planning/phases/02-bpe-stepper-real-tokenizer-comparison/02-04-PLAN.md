## Phase Goal

**As a** reader with no programming background, **I want** the stepper to show me the counts, the winner, the reason and the new vocabulary at every step, **so that** I can see for myself how a tokenizer's list of pieces is built.

Source: ROADMAP criteria 1 and 2, D-02 to D-07, UI-SPEC sections 1 and "Island lifecycle", Revision R-02.

## Flagged assumptions and planner choices

- Chip anatomy: the stepper gets its own `Chip.svelte` that takes the palette from the existing CSS variables (`--chip-N-fill/line`, so the palette is not duplicated). `TokenDisplay.svelte` keeps its own chip rules because it is user-owned (uncommitted edit) and byte-based; the UI-SPEC allows either a shared stylesheet or a `Chip.svelte` (Components row 3).
- Hook rule for every later plan: do not reuse the Phase 1 hooks `data-chip`, `data-table`, `data-live`, `data-status`, `data-msg`, `data-playground`; Phase 1 specs address them without scoping in places, and this plan scopes the ones that become ambiguous.
- Prose placement: the "three ways real tokenizers differ" sentence goes to "How this tokenizer works" in plan 02-09 (see plan 02-01), not right after the stepper.
