## Phase Goal

**As a** reader with no programming background, **I want** to pick a text and see how the toy tokenizer and two real tokenizers cut it, with a note on what to notice, **so that** I learn that different tokenizers cut the same text differently.

Source: ROADMAP criteria 3 and 5, D-08, D-10 to D-13, D-15 (stacked rows, one example at a time, per UI-SPEC section 2), TOK-07, TOK-09.

## Flagged assumptions and planner choices

- UI-SPEC R-02: BERT subline says "removes accents"; legend `[UNK]` sentence says "that word or character" (question A3 of plan 02-01).
- The comparison island imports the committed fixture directly instead of receiving it as a prop, so the data is bundled once in a cached chunk and is not serialised into the page HTML.
- The own-text block ("Try your own text") is plan 02-08. This plan leaves no placeholder or promise text for it.
- Source links: the only anchor in this plan is the same-origin link to the licence notices; `e2e/post.spec.ts` test (g) ("no links in the post column") is amended to allow anchors only inside `[data-source]` regions.
- Phase 1 hook rule (plan 02-04): this plan introduces `data-compare`, `data-row`, `data-row-count`, `data-note`, `data-source`, `data-alt-tables`, `data-announce` and reuses none of the Phase 1 hooks except the chip attribute `data-chip` inside TokenDisplay (specs scope it to `[data-compare]`).
