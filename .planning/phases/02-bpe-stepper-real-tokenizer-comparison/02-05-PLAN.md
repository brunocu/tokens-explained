## Phase Goal

**As a** reader with no programming background, **I want** to see the same texts cut by the toy, GPT-2 and BERT, and the same sentence in six languages, with a plain note on what to notice, **so that** I see that tokenizers differ and why.

Source: ROADMAP criteria 3 to 5, D-10, D-12 to D-14, UI-SPEC sections 2 and 3 as amended by R-02.

## Flagged assumptions (see plan 02-01)

- A1: whole UDHR Article 1 for all six languages. If the user declined, replace the strings in Task 2 by the chosen sentences and keep the same source-record fields.
- A5: the Arabic string comes from un.org only; the human-check in Task 2 asks the author to compare it with the OHCHR Arabic page once.
- Counts for GPT-2 and BERT are pinned from RESEARCH Gap 7 (computed 2026-10-06 with the pinned files). The toy counts are not pinned (they depend on merges.json); the freshness test covers them.
