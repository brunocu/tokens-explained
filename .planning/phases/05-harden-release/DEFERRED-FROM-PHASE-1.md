## 3. Other Phase 1 facts Phase 5 should know

- The dev environment is a Nix flake (`flake.nix`, `flake.lock`, `.envrc`); nixpkgs is pinned by revision `c59305bab2065cfecc4944690d9eedbb56f3a9fa`. `@playwright/test` is an exact pin that must equal the flake's `playwright-driver` version; `scripts/check-env.mjs` enforces it. Bumping Playwright means bumping the nixpkgs revision and the npm pin together.
- `playwright.config.ts` variables: `E2E_PORT` (default 4321), `E2E_SECONDARY` (set by `test:e2e:secondary`). `site.config.mjs` variables: `SITE_URL`, `SITE_BASE`, `SITE_OUT_DIR`.

- Routing is root-level (`/what-is-a-token/`, home at `/` with a canonical link). Once published, the post URL is a public contract (GitHub Pages cannot issue a 301). Phase 4 replaces only `src/pages/index.astro`; a post slug must not collide with a static page name because `[slug]` and static pages share the root namespace.
- The Phase 1 UI-SPEC and research still mention the GitHub Pages sub-path, iOS Safari and a `/posts/` route in places. The amendment in `01-CONTEXT.md` D-15 and the plan notes take precedence; those two documents were not edited.
