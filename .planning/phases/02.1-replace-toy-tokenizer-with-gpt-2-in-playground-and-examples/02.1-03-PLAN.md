## Goal

The playground's Playwright coverage passes again on chromium with GPT-2, and covers the new loading, failure and retry behaviour.

Source: D-05, D-06, D-07, D-08, D-01; RESEARCH G5 and G6; EVALUATION section 5.7 (corrected by RESEARCH G6 for the TextEncoder hook).

## Flagged assumptions and planner choices

- `comparison.spec.ts`, `comparison-load.spec.ts` and `languages.spec.ts` are NOT touched here; they depend on the comparison redesign and are rewritten in plans 02.1-06 (comparison-load) and 02.1-07 (comparison and languages). They may be red while this plan runs and are excluded from its verify command. `a11y-phase2.spec.ts` is likewise left to plan 02.1-07.
- `privacy.spec.ts` test 2 is rewritten here because its premise (no tokenizer request on the post page) is already false after plan 02.1-01. Its new form holds before and after the comparison redesign: exactly one request for the GPT-2 file, none for the BERT file, none caused by the picker or the tables.
- Expected values are derived by running the page or the engine; the executor must not copy numbers from the old specs or the evaluation.
