## Goal

A reader who opens the post sees the default text cut by the real GPT-2 tokenizer at once (server-rendered), the textbox unlocks as soon as the 0.5 MB file arrives, and nothing about a slow download ever makes the page look broken. This is the tracer of Phase 2.1: one path (default text, then live GPT-2) through every layer the phase touches: fixture generation, build-time props, hydration, loader and browser.

Source: ROADMAP Phase 2.1 goal; D-03, D-04, D-05, D-07, D-08, D-10; RESEARCH G1, G4, G5.

## Flagged assumptions and planner choices

- Example chips stay enabled during loading and read their tokens from a build-time lookup of six texts (the default plus the five examples), as the planning brief resolved RESEARCH Open Question 1. The island never imports comparison.json (RESEARCH Pitfall 6): the `.astro` wrapper slices it.
- The fixture gains a top-level `defaultText` entry and per-tokenizer download sizes **additively**. The toy rows and the combined download figure stay until plans 02.1-05 (the toy rows) and 02.1-06 (the combined figure) remove them, so every existing consumer keeps compiling.

- Phase 5 note (D-04): auto-load adds the GPT-2 file (0.55 MB gzip, 0.44 MB brotli) to every visit, so Phase 5 criterion 4 ("the initial bundle fits its budget") must be read with this in mind. The executor records this sentence in the SUMMARY; plan 02.1-09 writes it into the Phase 5 hand-off file.
