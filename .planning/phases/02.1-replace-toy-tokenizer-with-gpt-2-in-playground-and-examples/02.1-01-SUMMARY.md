# Phase 2.1 Plan 01: GPT-2 in the Playground Summary

**The playground now cuts text with live GPT-2: the default text is server-rendered from the committed fixture, the island hydrates at page load and fetches the 0.5 MB file once, and the textbox unlocks on arrival while a slow download never trips the failure watcher.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 6 min
- **Started:** 2026-10-07T15:07:00Z
- **Completed:** 2026-10-07T15:13:00Z
- **Tasks:** 2 (Task 1 tracer, Task 2 TDD)
- **Files modified:** 11 (1 created, 10 modified)

## Accomplishments

- Fixture generation gained `defaultText` (text plus GPT-2 rows) and per-tokenizer download sizes (`meta.download.gpt2`, `.bert`), regenerated by `npm run precompute:comparison`, and covered by the existing freshness test plus three new assertions.
- `PlaygroundSection.astro` builds a six-entry lookup (default text and five examples) from the fixture, throws a build error naming any missing example, and hydrates the island with `client:load`.
- `Playground.svelte` drops the toy tokenizer, loads GPT-2 in `onMount`, keeps `data-state="ready"` from hydration, and reports the download through `data-tokenizer`. The textbox is `readonly` until the download is ready; example chips, view toggle, peek and Reset keep working from props.
- `loadTokenizer` keeps one memoised promise per URL, evicts a rejected one, and exports `resetTokenizerCache()`; it is still the only fetch site (`check:repo` OK).
- New tracer spec `e2e/playground-load.spec.ts` (4 tests) passes on chromium, including a 9.5 second delayed download that never reads as failed.

## Decisions Made

- Memo key is the full tokenizer URL (`${base}tokenizers/${file}`), unique per base and id, instead of a separately joined string; behaviour is identical to the plan's "base and id joined".
- Eviction compares `cache.get(key) === attempt`, so a failed old attempt can never delete a newer entry.
- The fixture change is additive; toy rows and the combined download figure stay for plans 02.1-05 and 02.1-06.
