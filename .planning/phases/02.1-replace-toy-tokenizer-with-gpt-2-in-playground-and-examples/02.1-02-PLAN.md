## Goal

The playground handles its one new failure mode in plain words, never tells a reader that an end-of-text marker "gave up", and the display-layer unit tests prove their invariants on real GPT-2 output.

Source: D-06, D-07, D-01, D-03; RESEARCH G2, G6, G7; EVALUATION sections 5.5 and 5.6.

## Flagged assumptions and planner choices

- RESEARCH G7 asks that the legend prop "become a kind". The planner keeps `special` (the [UNK] sentence) as is, so the comparison, languages and own-text callers keep compiling, and adds a second flag `marker` for the special-token sentence. A pure helper `specialKinds(tokens)` in src/core/display.ts decides which apply, so TokenDisplay, and later OwnText (plan 02.1-06), use one rule.
- The retry button takes focus only after a retry the reader pressed fails again. On the first failure at page load focus is not moved (the reader has not acted yet; moving focus would scroll the page). The message sits in a status region so it is announced.
- Unit tests load GPT-2 with a top-level `await buildGpt2()` so describe-time tokenisation keeps working.
