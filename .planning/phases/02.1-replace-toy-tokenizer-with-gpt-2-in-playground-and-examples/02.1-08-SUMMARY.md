# Phase 2.1 Plan 08: Delete the retired toy tokenizer Summary

**The toy tokenizer, its merge list, pre-split helper, offline trainer and corpus are gone; isValidUtf8 moved to chars.ts, the hand-computed Sennrich tie-break golden now runs on trainOnPieces in the stepper tests, and check-core.mjs smoke-tests GPT-2 natively in Node.**

## Accomplishments

- Task 1 (21ffe02): moved `isValidUtf8` (strict decoder, `ignoreBOM: true`) into `src/core/chars.ts` and pointed `realTokenizer.ts` and `compare/fixture.ts` at it; added tests for ASCII and two-, three- and four-byte characters, a byte-order mark kept as three bytes, and a lone continuation byte, a cut-off sequence and an overlong form. Re-homed the golden as test (y) in a describe block renamed "the stepper's one tie-break comparator" and dropped the trainer-library comparison. Replaced the toy half of `check-core.mjs` (GPT-2: "Hi" one token, "hello" one token, cat emoji gives partial tokens whose bytes join to the emoji's UTF-8 bytes). Trimmed comments in `bpe.ts`, `stepper.ts` and `examples.ts`.
- Task 2 (066be13): `git rm` of the eleven files, removed the `train:toy` and `prepare:corpus` scripts, trimmed `types.ts` to `Token`, and swept for dangling references.
- Mutation check: flipping the comparator in `bpe.ts` to `b.key - a.key` made six stepper tests fail, including the re-homed golden (y); the comparator was then restored (`git diff` shows only the comment changes in `bpe.ts`).
