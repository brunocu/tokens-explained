# Phase 2.1 Plan 03: Playground end-to-end coverage on GPT-2 Summary

**The playground's Playwright specs pass again on chromium with numbers re-derived from GPT-2, a shared `openPlaygroundReady` helper removes the locked-textbox flakiness, the privacy spec pins the one-GPT-2-request budget, and failure, retry, 404 and accessibility of the new states are covered.**

## Accomplishments

- `e2e/helpers.ts` exports `openPlaygroundReady(page, path = "./")`: goto, scroll the card into view, wait for `data-state="ready"`, then `data-tokenizer="ready"`, return the card. Adopted in playground, controls, a11y, smoke, post, states, frames, resilience, privacy and the phone spec. The deliberate "loading" and "failed" observers (smoke (a), the lifecycle tests) keep their intent; states (e) gained the second wait before checking the textbox is editable.
- Numbers re-derived from the engine (scratch probe with `buildGpt2`, `groupBytes`, `tableCell`) and then confirmed in the browser:
  - 男: 2 chips (`E7 94`, `B7`), 1 dashed cut. 日本語: 6 chips in 3 frames, rows "Bytes 1 to 2 of 3 of 日", "Byte 3 of 3 of 日", and so on.
  - 🙂 alone: 2 rows ("Bytes 1 to 2 of 4", "Bytes 3 to 4 of 4"), bytes `F0 9F` and `99 82`.
  - Non-English example: 3 frames (男, こ, ち), 6 hatched chips, 4 whole chips (女, ん, に, は).
  - Resilience EXACT and FRAMES tables: emoji example 9 chips with 3 partial and 1 frame; zwj-family 10 and 5 frames; flag 6 and 2; chinese 11; devanagari 12; arabic and hebrew 1 frame each; lone surrogate, zero-width space and precomposed é now 0 frames (GPT-2 holds them as whole tokens). Freeze guard: 2,000 chips instead of 3,000.
- `privacy.spec.ts` test 2 now asserts exactly one request whose path ends in `/tokenizers/gpt2.json`, none containing `bert`, no other tokenizer request, and nothing at all after using the comparison picker, the alternative tables and the source details; all requests are same-origin. Test 1 uses the helper and still asserts zero requests while typing, using examples, toggles and the table.
- `playground-load.spec.ts` gained tests (6) to (10): blocked file keeps chips, counts and legend with a locked textbox and working example buttons; Try again refetches (request count 1 then 2) and unlocks, typing then works and the live region says ready; a failed retry returns focus to Try again; a 404 ends in the same failed state; with the file held on a promise gate each of the five examples shows engine-derived chips and Reset restores the default, with a single request in total.
- `a11y.spec.ts` gained axe scans (WCAG 2.2 A and AA) of the loading state (file held) and the failed state (file aborted), in light and dark.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] controls.spec (f) assumed the emoji is cut into pieces**
- **Found during:** Task 1 verify
- **Issue:** The test typed "hi 🙂" and waited for a partial chip. GPT-2 holds " 🙂" as one whole token (id 32485), so there is no partial chip and the test timed out. This is a real GPT-2 difference, not a wait problem.
- **Fix:** Typed "hi 🎉" instead (" 🎉" is cut into three partial pieces, "hi" stays whole), keeping the test's intent (a mix of whole and partial chips) with a comment explaining why.
- **Files modified:** `e2e/controls.spec.ts`
- **Commit:** `649a9fe`

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `phone-width.secondary.spec.ts` expected 3,000 chips**
- The file is in the plan's Task 1 and Task 2 lists but the 1,000-CJK-character count (3,000 under the old tokenizer) was a number to re-derive: it is 2,000 under GPT-2. Changed together with the resilience freeze guard. Secondary phone project run: 6 of 6 pass (not gating).
- **Commit:** `274d64b`
