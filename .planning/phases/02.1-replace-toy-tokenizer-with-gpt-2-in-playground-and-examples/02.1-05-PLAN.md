## Goal

The comparison card, the languages table and the languages "look inside" view compare GPT-2 with BERT only, and every sentence beside them is true of those two rows.

Source: D-01, D-18; EVALUATION sections 5.3, 5.4, 7.1; ROADMAP Phase 2 criteria 3 to 5 as amended.

## Flagged assumptions and planner choices

- This plan removes the retired tokenizer from every display consumer in one cut, because `FixtureRows` and the `TokenRows` row type are shared; splitting the cut would leave the type-check red. The own-text block (`OwnText.svelte`) gets only the removal here; its new load flow (D-09) is plan 02.1-06.
- `TokenRows` accepts `{ gpt2: Token[]; bert?: Token[] }` and skips a row whose tokens are not supplied, so plan 02.1-06 needs no further change to it.
- The combined download figure in the fixture stays until plan 02.1-06 (the own-text block still reads it); only the retired tokenizer's rows and `meta` entry go now.
- Notes are written by reading the regenerated rows, not from the evaluation's drafts; the executor checks each sentence against `comparison.json`.
- Comparison e2e specs stay red until plan 02.1-07.
