# Phase 02.1 Plan 10: One Try again recovers every GPT-2 widget (G-02.1-3) Summary

**The loader now announces each successful download in memory, and a small shared controller lets a Try again in the playground or the comparison card clear the error in the other widget, silently, with one request per attempt.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 15 minutes
- **Tasks:** 3 of 3
- **Files:** 2 created, 5 modified

## Accomplishments

- `onTokenizerLoaded` in `src/components/loadTokenizer.ts`: listeners per base-plus-id key, called once per successful download with the same tokenizer object the loader resolves with, never on failure, errors swallowed, cleared by `resetTokenizerCache`. The loader stays the only file in `src` with a network call; `FETCH_ALLOWED_FILES` is unchanged.
- `src/components/tokenizerDownload.ts`: `createTokenizerDownload(id, baseUrl, handlers)` with `start(retried)` and `dispose()`. `onReady(tokenizer, "own" | "other")` tells the widget whether it may announce and move focus.
- Playground and comparison card both use the controller (created in `onMount`, disposed in the cleanup). The card tracks a `retriedAttempt` flag so a page-load success still announces and focuses nothing. BERT is untouched.
- Tests: 7 new loader tests, 7 controller tests (the plan listed six cases; the "recovers by its own press after the other widget already succeeded" case was added), and the e2e spec (c2) updated plus (c2b) and (c2c) added.
