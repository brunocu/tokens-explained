## Phase Goal

**As a** reader using a screen reader or the table alternative, **I want** the same fact the frames show, which bytes belong to which character, in words, **so that** the grouping is not only a picture.

Source: UI-SPEC revision R-01 "Copy added or changed by R-01" and "Test impact", on top of the ROADMAP Phase 1 goal. Planning decisions PD-03 to PD-06 are recorded in `01-05-PLAN.md`; PD-04 items 5 and 6 are implemented here.
