## Phase Goal

**As a** reader with no programming background, **I want to** open the "What is a token?" post (served locally in Phase 1, deployed in Phase 5) and play with a tokenizer in my browser, **so that** I come away with a correct mental model of what a token is.

Source: ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal plus PROJECT Core Value.

This plan's slice: the reader opens the post at its root-level URL and reads the complete post, whose every quoted example comes from the real tokenizer, with the playground in the middle, and the whole static build is verified accessible, resilient and base-portable.
