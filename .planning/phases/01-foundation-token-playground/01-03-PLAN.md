## Phase Goal

**As a** reader with no programming background, **I want to** open the "What is a token?" post (served locally in Phase 1, deployed in Phase 5) and play with a tokenizer in my browser, **so that** I come away with a correct mental model of what a token is.

Source: ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal plus PROJECT Core Value.

This plan's slice: the reader types, picks examples, flips the views and sees colored, outlined, glyph-marked tokens with counts, a screen-reader and keyboard alternative, honest loading and failure states and reduced-motion respect, in a desktop browser (narrow layouts keep the UI-SPEC responsive rules but are secondary).
