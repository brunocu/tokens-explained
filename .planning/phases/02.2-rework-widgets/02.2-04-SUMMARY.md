# Phase 2.2 Plan 04: Stepper rework Summary

**The BPE stepper is a 720px two-column widget with an explanation region, a words region and a pair-count region, compact two-line chips, neutral lifecycle copy and an import-and-tag MDX placement. Its height is proven constant across all 14 stages in three browsers.**

## What was done

- **Task 1** (`11b4fb4`). Rebuilt `BpeStepper.svelte` as a `section` with the `widget--stepper` grid (5fr/7fr, 720px). The left column holds the status line, stage line and progress squares, the four buttons, the explanation region (the one `role="status"` live region per stage, no minimum height), the "Next: ... becomes one new token" callout and the two stats. The right column holds the block label, the elastic words region, the pair table in its own non-elastic region (`padded={false}`) and the legend. Off-scale sizes snapped. `Chip.svelte` now uses `stack-chip stack-chip--compact` from `widget.css`. `ScrollRegion.svelte` gained `class` and `padded`. The MDX imports `BpeStepper` and places `<BpeStepper client:visible />` under "## Build a vocabulary yourself" after the word-for-word lead-in. `StepperSection.astro` is deleted and removed from `PostLayout.astro`. The content test's headings, markers, count loop, lead-in pin and position tests moved to the island tag together.
- **Task 2** (`afe2cd2`). Migrated the stepper and lifecycle specs to the neutral wording and the `stack-chip-text` / `stack-chip-id` classes, extended test (f) to cover the double border, a learned piece's number and the plain single-byte outline, and added eight `@stepper` layout tests. Measured the pair-table maximum.

The phone secondary test (e) already used the region selector and passes unchanged.

## Measurement: pair-table maximum height at 720px

Words-region room at 1440x900 was 302px with the table at 240px and 342px with it at 200px (the table is 40px shorter, so the words region gains 40px). Chromium, Firefox and WebKit agree to within a few pixels: WebKit shows 293 for the table at stages 11 and 12, and Firefox's table is 3px taller (297) and its merge 1 and 2 word strips 1px taller (385, 373).

Chosen maximum: **200px**. The plan's rule was to keep 200px if merges 1 and 2 stop scrolling, and otherwise keep 200px anyway if it reduces overflow (never below 160px, never above 720px height). It reduces overflow (merge 3 stops scrolling), so 200px stays. **Merges 1 and 2 still scroll the words region by 42px and 30px** (Chromium). Reaching them would need the table at about 160px or a taller widget; the plan forbids raising the height, and 160px would leave merge 1 scrolling by 2px, so this is reported rather than forced.

Legend height measured at 1440 wide: about 59px (the research estimate was 45px, unmeasured).
