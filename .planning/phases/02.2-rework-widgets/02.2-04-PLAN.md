## Goal

Convert the BPE stepper to the new widget at the user-decided 720px height, with the compact two-line chip, regions, neutral wording, MDX placement and the height-across-stages proof.

Source: D-01 to D-06, D-08, D-14; UI-SPEC "2. BPE Stepper", "Shared Chip Format", "Off-scale values to snap", "Lifecycle, Status and Neutral Wording"; RESEARCH G-2 (stepper table); binding user decision 1.

## Flagged assumptions and planner choices

- Binding user decision 1: the stepper height is 720px, not 680px, and the pair table's maximum height drops from 240px to about 200px if that lets merges 1 and 2 fit without the words region scrolling. The research measured 354px and 342px of word strip at those merges against about 319px of room at 720px, so the cut may be needed. The executor re-measures: first at 720px with 240px, then with 200px; keep 200px if it removes the scroll at merges 1 and 2 (not below 160px). If the words region still scrolls at merges 1 or 2 after the cut, keep the cut, keep 720px, record each stage's result in the SUMMARY, and report it; do not raise the height past 720px.
- `ScrollRegion.svelte` (plan 02) gains two optional props here, `class` (extra class names) and `padded` (default true; false for regions that hold a table and need no inner padding). Plans 05 and 06 reuse them.
- The legend estimate in the research was about 45px and unmeasured; the executor measures it.
- The latest merged piece keeps its 350ms pop animation; it is removed by the global reduced-motion rule and nothing is added.
