## Flagged assumptions and planner choices

- The research measured the token rows plus the legend at up to 486px and a rows region of 542px at 720px, so the rows always fit, and only the table details (44px) and the provenance (about 130px) push the region content to about 680px for the two longest examples. This plan accepts that scroll (the UI-SPEC placement, which keeps the provenance with the results) and adds a guard that the rows and legend are fully visible without scrolling. Moving the provenance below the region was the research's alternative; it is not taken because the UI-SPEC places it inside, and the user has not asked to change it.
- The picker's first example is the default one ("A rare long word"), which is also the longest rows case; the default state therefore shows a small scroll in the rows region (provenance below the fold). That is expected and is recorded in the SUMMARY.
- Order inside the rows region follows the UI-SPEC: the rows, the shared legend, the "BERT is on its way" line when relevant, the tables details, the provenance.
- The scroll position of the rows region resets on a deliberate choice (picker, "Use the example text again") through a counter, not while typing.
